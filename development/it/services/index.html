    Consulting & Professional Services

    BX Docs is free, open source, and built to be used without anyone's help - but if you'd rather have the team who builds it do the work, Ortus Solutions offers professional services and consulting around it. That covers documentation sites, but also any other static site: BX Docs is a general-purpose static site generator, not just a docs tool, and our services cover it the same way.

    Email consulting@ortussolutions.com View All Ortus Services

    What we can help with

    Docs & static site builds

    A documentation site, a marketing site, a blog, or any other static site - designed, built, and deployed with BX Docs from the ground up.

    Migrations

    Moving an existing GitBook or mkdocs project - or another docs/static-site tool entirely - over to BX Docs, content and structure intact.

    Custom themes & plugins

    A theme that matches your brand, or a BoxLang module plugin for a workflow BX Docs doesn't cover out of the box.

    Training & support

    Get your team up to speed on BX Docs, BoxLang, and the wider Ortus ecosystem, or set up ongoing support for a site already in production.

    Why Ortus Solutions

    Ortus Solutions builds and maintains BoxLang, BX Docs, and the rest of the BoxLang ecosystem - nobody knows this tooling better than the people who wrote it. Professional services with us aren't limited to BX Docs, either: see the full range, from staff augmentation to production support, at ortussolutions.com/services.

    Get in touch

    Email consulting@ortussolutions.com to talk about your project - what you're building, your timeline, and where you'd like a hand.
